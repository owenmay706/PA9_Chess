Instructions:
Chess is a two-player strategy game played on an 8×8 board with 64 squares, where each player starts with
16 pieces: one king, one queen, two rooks, two bishops, two knights, and eight pawns. White always moves
first, and players take turns moving one piece at a time. Each piece has its own movement rules: the king
moves one square in any direction, the queen moves any number of squares in any direction, rooks move
horizontally or vertically, bishops move diagonally, knights move in an L-shape and can jump over pieces,
and pawns move forward one square while capturing diagonally. The goal of the game is to defeat the
opponent by capturing their king.

Multiplayer:
For multiplayer, when you press m to play multiplayer, you will have to switch the the terminal and follow the
instructions there. You will have to enter the IP address of the other player and then you will be able to play
chess with them. The game will be turn based, so you will have to wait for the other player to make their move
before you can make yours. The game will end when one player captures the other player's king. If you want to 
play again, you can press 'r' to restart the game or 'esc' to exit on both game instances even if the screen
doesn't prompt it.


Note: The game ends when a player physically captures the opponent's king. Also, this game does not currently
use specialized rules like promotion, castling, or en passant :)
